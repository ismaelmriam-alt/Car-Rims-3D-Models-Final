# Car Rims 3D model sources and licensing

The 10 selected vehicle names in the app use **unbranded body-style preview assets**, not exact manufacturer CAD/OEM replicas. The preview model must never be used to infer wheel fitment. Fitment status and specifications are supplied independently by the vehicle catalogue.

All remote models below are listed by Innerscene as original CC0 models, allowing commercial use, modification and redistribution without attribution. The local Ferrari/supercar preview is the same CC0 generic mid-engine model supplied in the project workspace.

## Important limitations

- The preview assets are generic/unbranded. The app does not claim that they reproduce the exact body panels, dimensions or details of the named car.
- Remote GLB assets load from Innerscene at runtime. The browser needs internet access to display them.
- Some source models omit cabin, suspension or powertrain detail; the source page describes the model-specific limitations.
- No fitment values are inferred from the 3D model. Models without verified wheel data remain marked as unverified in the app.
